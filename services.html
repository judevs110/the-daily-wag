Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

We visit you and your dog at your home — completely free. It's a chance for your dog to get comfortable with us and for us to learn their routine, likes, and dislikes before the first walk.
We always aim for consistency. For solo walks especially, we assign one primary walker. In case of illness or holiday, we introduce a backup walker in advance so your dog is never surprised.
Keys are stored in a secure key safe with no identifying address labels. They are returned to you immediately if you cancel the service. Many clients prefer a coded key safe at their door instead.
All walkers are Pet First Aid certified. You'll be contacted immediately. We carry basic first aid supplies and know the location of local emergency vets. Our insurance covers veterinary costs arising from any incident in our care.
Yes — dogs need exercise rain or shine! We walk in light rain and cold weather. We only cancel in extreme weather (storms, ice, extreme heat) and will always communicate with you in advance if a walk needs to be adapted.
