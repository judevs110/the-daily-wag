Who We Are

About Us

Born from a Love of Dogs & Windsor

The Daily Wag started in 2019 when our founder, Jamie, noticed a gap in truly personalised, trustworthy dog walking in Windsor. Having grown up with dogs and spending years volunteering at local shelters, Jamie set out to build a service where every dog is treated like family.

We're not a faceless agency — we're a small, dedicated team of dog lovers who live and walk the same streets and parks as you. Every walker goes through rigorous training, background checks, and a supervised trial period before walking independently.

Whether your dog needs a vigorous run through Windsor Great Park or a gentle amble along the Thames, we match them with the right walker and the right route every single time.
